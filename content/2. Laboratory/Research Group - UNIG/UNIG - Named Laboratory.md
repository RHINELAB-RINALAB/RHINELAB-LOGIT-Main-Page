Since many of the laboratory function would take into effect and the announcement thereof, there exists the need for **core operational laboratories** that would be of absolute importance to the institution there. They are what is titled **named laboratories**, falling under *UNIG-X* classification, and their roles would be mostly to provide theoretical, practical, and following up of such being logistics, documentations and knowledge archives of what is there to follow and observe. In a given sense, they *create RHINELAB architecture*. 
___
Because of such role, there exists, for now, three fundamental laboratories discretization - `RHINE@LOGIT`, i.e. *UNIG-02*, `RHINE@MTAIL`, *UNIG-01*, and `RHINE@GTPR`, *UNIG-03* on that matter. For preliminary functions and details about them, see [[RL.2. Laboratory Structure]].
