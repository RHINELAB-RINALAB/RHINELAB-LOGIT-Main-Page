# Call for support
We require, for further operations and 100% operational efficiency (or at least we consider it so), some amount of monetary or **capabilities**, like computational resource. While we are applying (or at least in attempts to do so), under [**Open Source Collective**](https://opencollective.com/), we also welcome, especially so, personal fund and donation there is. 

For now, such can be asked directly to Principle Director i.e. the founder himself, listed in [[Contact]], however, we would want to formalize and institutionalize this in the future for records and holding thereof, for contributes related to such.
